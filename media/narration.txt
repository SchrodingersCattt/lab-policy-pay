Research teams receive spending rules in documents, while their receipts arrive separately. Ledger Lab connects those two moments.

Upload a policy notice. Local OCR reads the document and prepares its text for the AI assistant.

The assistant extracts the monthly budget, approval threshold and single expense limit, with the original evidence in a reviewable table.

A request for two hundred and one dollars is blocked. Rule three sets the limit at two hundred dollars, so this request is one dollar over.

Now upload a receipt. OCR and AI extract the amount and description, and the request is checked against the confirmed rules.

After approval, PayPal Sandbox Payouts sends the reimbursement. Ledger Lab checks the matching payout item before marking it paid.

The searchable AG Grid ledger keeps the evidence, policy decision and payment status together.
